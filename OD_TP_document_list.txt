MOTOR CLAIM DOCUMENT LIST - OWN DAMAGE (OD) vs THIRD PARTY (TP)
================================================================
Prepared: 2026-10-06
Sources:
  1. Client attribute sheet (31 document rows, pasted)
  2. bgi_v2_with_chuck/src/sqc/skills/instructions/document_classification.md
     (65 classifier labels)
  3. Pipeline base checklists in triage.py (_REQUIRED_DOCS) and
     supervisor.py (_generate_document_checklist)

Legend:  [M] = mandatory / core for that claim type
         [C] = conditional (depends on case: theft, death, injury, commercial
               vehicle, total loss, financed vehicle, MACT filed, etc.)
         Classifier label shown in brackets, e.g. (fir)


----------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION B - OWN DAMAGE (OD) CLAIM DOCUMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Core set (all Section A items that apply) PLUS:

 1. Repair Estimate / Job Card / Garage Tax Invoice (repair_estimate)      [M]
 2. Payment Slip / Receipt / Discharge-Satisfaction Voucher (payment_slip) [M]
 3. Towing / Recovery / Flatbed bill               (towing)                [C]
 4. NCB Certificate / NCB recovery letter          (ncb_certificate)       [C]
 5. Trade Certificate (Form 16/17)                 (trade_certificate)     [C] dealer / unregistered vehicle
 6. Sale Certificate (Form 21)                     (sale_certificate)      [C] new vehicle / total loss / IDV check
 7. Certificate of Compliance / Roadworthiness (Form 22) (compliance_certificate) [C]
 8. Vehicle Purchase Invoice (dealer sale invoice) (purchase_invoice)      [C] total loss / IDV check
 9. Delivery Note / Challan                        (delivery_note)         [C]
10. Registration Application (Form 20)             (registration_application) [C]
11. Transfer of Ownership (Form 29/30)             (transfer_of_ownership) [C] ownership mismatch
12. Hypothecation Form (Form 34/35)                (hypothecation_form)    [C] financed vehicle / total loss payout
13. NOC from financier / RTO                       (noc)                   [C] theft / total loss / transfer
14. Untraced / Non-traceable Final Report          (untraced_report)       [C] theft claim
15. Seizure Memo                                   (seizure_memo)          [C] police seized vehicle
16. Charge Sheet / Final Report                    (charge_sheet)          [C] theft / accident with police case

Pipeline hard-coded OD base checklist (for reference):
  triage.py      : CLAIM_APPLICATION, DRIVING_LICENSE, RC_BOOK
  supervisor.py  : FIR, RC Book, Driving License, Workshop Estimate
